# Cardiac Arrest / CPR Protocol

## Emergency Response
1. **VERIFY UNRESPONSIVENESS:** Check carotid pulse and telemetry readout in **Monitoring** tab.
2. **START CPR:** Begin chest compressions (100-120 bpm, 30 compressions / 2 breaths).
3. **AED DEPLOYMENT:** Retrieve automated external defibrillator from Sector A locker.
4. **EPINEPHRINE:** Prepare 1mg IV injection according to medical payload guidelines.
